QuicorDay

Privacy Policy

Effective date · Last updated: August 24, 2026

🔒

In short: recordings and calendar data are not sent to a developer-operated server.

Core recording, playback, and organization features work offline. On iPhone and iPad, Google Mobile Ads and UMP use the network when optional rewarded-ad availability or consent is handled. App Store or Google Play services are also used for purchasing or restoring Pro.

01 · Personal data we collect

The developer does not directly collect account information

The QuicorDay developer does not directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or location data, and the app has no registration or login. If you choose an iOS rewarded ad, Google's advertising SDK may process the ad-related data described below.

02 · Data stored on your device

What remains on your devices

The data below is stored only on your iPhone, Apple Watch, Android phone, or Galaxy Watch. It is not accessible to the QuicorDay developer or other third parties.

DataStorage locationPurpose
Audio recordings, recording time, duration, and organization statusOn-device IndexedDBPlayback and calendar organization
Theme color, recording quality, post-recording date assignment, and other preferencesLocal device storageApp personalization and recorder configuration
App-lock PIN or patternStored on-device after SHA-256 hashingApp lock and Secret Calendar protection
Biometric authentication result (Face ID, Touch ID, or Android fingerprint/face authentication)Handled by the device; the app receives only the resultUnlocking the app

App-lock PINs and patterns are stored only as SHA-256 hashes, not as the original values. The original PIN or pattern therefore cannot be recovered from the stored value. Biometric authentication is handled only by iOS LocalAuthentication or Android BiometricPrompt. QuicorDay never accesses face or fingerprint data and receives only the authentication result.

03 · Microphone access

The microphone is used only while recording

The microphone is activated only after you press the record button to begin a recording. Recorded audio remains on your device and is not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

04 · Apple Watch · Galaxy Watch

Watch data moves only between your paired devices

Apple Watch recordings are transferred to your paired iPhone using Apple's WatchConnectivity. Galaxy Watch recordings are transferred to your paired Android phone using the Wear OS Data Layer provided through Google Play services. Theme choices, the currently available recording-quality setting, and the post-recording date-assignment setting also move to the paired watch through the same device-to-device connection. QuicorDay does not operate a separate developer server for this transfer and does not collect or view the transferred data.

05 · Sharing · exporting

Sharing happens only when you choose it

When you choose Share, the recording is passed to the standard iOS or Android system share sheet and then only to the destination you select, such as Messages or another app. Further handling is governed by the selected service's own policy. Export saves the file in QuicorDay's app-specific storage without using the share sheet or sending the file outside the device.

06 · Third-party SDKs · analytics · advertising

Google SDKs are used only for optional iOS rewarded ads

The iPhone/iPad app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP) for optional rewarded ads and consent choices. Google may process IP address, device or app identifiers where permitted, diagnostics, performance data, and ad interactions for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and measurement. An ad appears only after you tap the rewarded-ad button; completing one grants one additional free calendar organization. No ad SDK is included in Apple Watch, Android, or Galaxy Watch builds. QuicorDay does not currently request ATT permission or IDFA tracking. See Google's Privacy Policy.

07 · In-app purchases

Apple or Google processes payment information

QuicorDay Pro purchases are processed only through Apple's standard App Store payment system (StoreKit) on iOS or Google Play Billing on Android. The QuicorDay developer does not directly collect or store payment-card information. Apple's or Google's privacy policy applies separately to the payment transaction.

08 · Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect children's personal data

QuicorDay does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the app does not send personal data to a developer-operated server, the same on-device protection applies regardless of age.

09 · Data deletion

Deleting the app deletes its locally stored data

Because all app data is stored on your device, deleting an individual recording in the app removes that recording. Deleting the app removes the app's locally stored data. No backup or copy exists on a QuicorDay developer server.

10 · Policy changes

This page contains the current policy

If this policy changes, this page and the in-app policy under Settings → Privacy Policy will be updated together, and the last-updated date shown above will be revised.
